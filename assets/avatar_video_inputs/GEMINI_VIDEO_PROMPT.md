# Gemini video prompt — ChorusFace 8s capture take

Drop the exported MP4 in this folder as `face_take.mp4` (or any `.mp4` / `.mov`).

Then run:

```bash
chorusface-capture --video assets/avatar_video_inputs/face_take.mp4 --output output/worlds/avatar/
```

---

## Prompt (copy everything below into Gemini)

**Duration:** exactly **8 seconds**  
**Aspect:** vertical or square portrait (9:16 or 1:1)  
**Camera:** locked, eye-level, facing the subject

---

Generate a single continuous **8-second** photorealistic close-up of **one adult woman**, front-facing, looking straight into the camera. Head-and-shoulders framing: forehead to upper chest fills most of the frame. Soft even studio lighting on both sides of the face, no harsh backlight, no silhouette. Sharp focus on the eyes and mouth. Skin texture natural and detailed. No sunglasses, no hat, no mask, no hands covering the face. Background is a simple soft gray or soft cream studio backdrop. The camera does not move. Only one person is visible.

Timed performance in this exact order (keep the head almost still; only the face and mouth move):

1. **0.0–1.0 s — Rest:** Neutral expression, mouth **fully closed with NO teeth visible**, lips sealed and relaxed, eyes open and looking at the lens. This must not look like a smile.
2. **1.0–2.0 s — Smile:** Clear closed-lip smile (corners of the mouth pull up; teeth mostly not shown). Still looking at the camera.
3. **2.0–3.5 s — Open:** Drop the jaw on a clear “ahh” so **upper teeth are clearly visible** inside the open mouth. Hold the open mouth long enough to read the teeth.
4. **3.5–8.0 s — Talk:** Speak clearly at a natural pace, looking at the camera, with visible lip and jaw motion:

   “Hello there. My name is Ava. Please buy me a puppy.”

Use open vowels and clear lip closures on “please,” “buy,” “puppy.” Do not turn the head in profile. Do not walk. No cutaways, no zoom, no text overlays, no captions, no logos.

---

## What we need (checklist for the take)

- [ ] Exactly one face, frontal
- [ ] Eyes clearly visible (no sunglasses)
- [ ] Sharp (no motion blur)
- [ ] Rest → Smile → Open (teeth) → Talk in order
- [ ] Open beat shows real teeth
- [ ] Face fills most of the frame
